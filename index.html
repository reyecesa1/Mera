Meet your stylist

The artist behind the chair

Elite Styles By MERA is a women-owned studio built on positivity, client satisfaction, and a genuine love for transformation. Every appointment is unhurried — a consultation, a plan, and a finish that turns heads.

Specializing in custom color, dimensional balayage, and premium keratin treatments, Mera brings years of hands-on expertise to a space designed to feel welcoming, professional, and completely yours.

Located inside Blue Lion Salon Studios, just off Kuykendahl Road in Spring, Texas.
